01About

About me

I started out in education before moving into IT and project management, and after a while, I realized I didn't want to choose between the two—I wanted to combine them. I've always loved figuring out what people actually need, helping them learn, and turning big ideas into things that actually work.

I've worked across teaching, tech, and project management, which taught me one main thing: I love solving people problems. I like uncovering where the friction is and coming up with clever, practical ways to fix it. Currently, I'm creating problem-based e-learning in Figma, using AI to work smarter and cut out unnecessary effort.
